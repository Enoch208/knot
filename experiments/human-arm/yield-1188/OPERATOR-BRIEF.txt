YIELD COMPARISON — TIMED TASK
=============================

You are being timed. That is the point of the exercise: we are measuring how
long this takes a person. Work at your normal pace. Do not rush, and do not
worry about being slow — a slow honest time is the useful result.

Take as long as you need. If you cannot finish, say so; that is also a result.


THE SITUATION
-------------

Someone holds 1,000 USDT deposited in a lending market on BNB Chain. They are
willing to leave it there for 365 days.

Their money is currently in:   venus-usdt-core

They want to know: should it stay where it is, or move to the other market?


YOUR FILE
---------

Everything you need is in one file:

    experiments/human-arm/yield-1188/input.json

It contains a frozen snapshot of two markets (Venus and Aave v3). For each
market the snapshot gives you, among other fields:

    baseRatePerPeriodRay     the interest rate, in "ray" units
    periodBasis              what period that rate is per
    availableLiquidityUnits  how much can be withdrawn right now
    entryCostUnits           cost to deposit into that market
    exitCostUnits            cost to withdraw from that market
    concentrationBps         this position's share of that market
    capacity / supplyState / withdrawalState / riskFlags

All amounts are integers in base units. USDT has 18 decimals, so
1 USDT = 1000000000000000000 units.


THE RULES THEY SET
------------------

    Holding horizon            365 days
    Withdrawal need            500 USDT must stay accessible
    Minimum liquidity floor    1,000 USDT
    Concentration cap          9000 bps
    Gas allowance              1 USDT
    Minimum improvement        1 USDT

Moving is only worth it if the gain, after paying to leave one market and
enter the other, beats that minimum improvement threshold.


WHAT TO HAND BACK
-----------------

Four answers:

    1. recommendedMarketId          which market should hold the money
    2. decision                     "stay" or "move"
    3. netBenefitUnitsOverHorizon   the gain from your recommendation over the
                                    365 days, in base units
    4. excludedMarketsAndWhy        any market you ruled out, and why. If you
                                    ruled none out, say so.

Also jot down, in your own words: what you did, anything that confused you,
anything you were unsure about, and roughly how you handled it. Rough notes
are fine. Being unsure is normal and worth recording.
