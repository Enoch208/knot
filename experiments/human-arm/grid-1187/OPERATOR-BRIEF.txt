THE SITUATION
-------------

Someone wants to run a grid trading strategy on the WBNB/USDT pair on BNB
Chain. They have supplied the parameters they want to use.

Your job: work out whether that grid is viable as specified, and if not,
which parameter is the problem.


YOUR FILE
---------

    experiments/human-arm/grid-1187/input.json

It contains the pair, the pool, the requested grid parameters, and a frozen
market snapshot. Amounts are integers in base units; both tokens have 18
decimals, so 1 token = 1000000000000000000 units.


WHAT TO WORK OUT
----------------

    1. decision                          is the grid viable as specified
    2. levelCount                        how many price levels the parameters imply
    3. totalCapitalCommittedQuoteUnits   total quote-token capital the ladder commits
    4. rejectedParametersAndWhy          any parameter you reject, and why. If none, say so.


WHAT YOU MAY USE
