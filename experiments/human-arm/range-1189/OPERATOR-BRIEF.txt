YIELD COMPARISON — TIMED TASK
=============================

You are being timed. That is the point of the exercise: we are measuring how
long this takes a person. Work at your normal pace. Do not rush, and do not
worry about being slow — a slow honest time is the useful result.

Take as long as you need. If you cannot finish, say so; that is also a result.


THE SITUATION
-------------

Someone holds a PancakeSwap v3 liquidity position on BNB Chain. They want
to know whether to leave it alone or rebalance it.

Your job: work out where the current price sits relative to their position,
and whether their own constraints permit an action.


YOUR FILE
---------

    experiments/human-arm/range-1189/input.json

It contains the position, the pool, the current tick, the position bounds,
and the constraints the owner set. Ticks are integers.


WHAT TO WORK OUT
----------------

    1. decision                    hold or rebalance
    2. currentTickRelativeToRange  where the current tick sits vs the bounds
    3. reason                      why, in one or two lines
    4. unavailableMetrics          anything the snapshot does not let you determine


WHAT YOU MAY USE
----------------

    The input.json file
    A calculator (or a spreadsheet)
    Public Venus documentation
    Public Aave documentation
    A block explorer


WHAT YOU MUST NOT USE
---------------------

    Any AI assistant of any kind
    KNOT, or anything belonging to KNOT
    Any answer to this task that you have seen before
    Anyone else's help

If you do end up using any of these, just say so — an assisted run recorded
honestly is useful. A run recorded as unaided when it was not is worthless to
us and worse than not doing it at all.


BEFORE YOU START
----------------

Tell the person who gave you this that you are ready. They start a timer, then
leave you alone. When you are done, tell them, and give them your four answers
and your notes.

Do not edit any file. Just hand back your answers.
